## 🏆 Objectives

*By the end of this class, you'll be able to&hellip;*

1. Identify the three clicks a demo must hit (syllabus, module, project)
1. Explain what stays SAMPLE vs what a live course would replace
1. Deliver a timed walkthrough without apologizing for the template

<!-- > -->

## [**5m**] Attendance &amp; Announcements

Studio week starts after this module. Final presentations are Wednesday, April 15 (SAMPLE term).

## [**15m**] ☀️ Warm Up

Three-question recap:

1. Where does the blank starter live?
2. Where does the filled sample live?
3. What command serves the filled site?

<!-- > -->

## [**25m**] 📚 TT: Overview

A demo is a product tour, not a memoir.

1. Syllabus — SAMPLE banner, 7-week table, outcomes
2. One module — agenda, activity, done state
3. Project — requirements table and rubric

Then one honest sentence: *If this were a live ACS class, I would replace X.* X is dates, Gradescope, or topic — not the Docsify shell.

<aside class="notes">
If Docsify is not running, start it before you talk. Dead air is not a teaching beat.
</aside>
