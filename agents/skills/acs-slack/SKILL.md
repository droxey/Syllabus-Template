# ACS Slack course channels

## Canon

Read `https://github.com/droxey/Syllabus-Template/blob/master/SLACK.md` (or `SLACK.md` in the course repo). Related: [TERM.md](https://github.com/droxey/Syllabus-Template/blob/master/TERM.md) day-1 launch.

## Channel naming

- Pattern: `#acs-xxxx` where `xxxx` is the **4-digit course code**.
- From repo prefix: `ACS-3220-...` → `#acs-3220`.
- Channels are usually **already created** — day-1 work is **invites only**.

## Invites

- Source: Dominican Ellucian roster CSV export.
- When Dani hands the CSV/list, add those people to `#acs-xxxx` (that handoff is the OK for invites).
- Do not invent a roster or scrape PII into lesson files.

## Channel map

- `#acs-*` and `#general` — student-facing
- `#faculty-acs` — faculty only (administrators watch — extra care)
- Assume other users are students unless known otherwise

## Send guardrails

1. Confirm with Dani **before any Slack send** (invites after CSV handoff are the exception Dani locked).
2. Never discuss grades or scores in Slack.
3. Never put private student feedback in channels.
4. Prefer drafts for Dani to approve when a message leaves under their name.
